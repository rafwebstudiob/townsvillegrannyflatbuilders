Townsville's planned southern suburbs, such as Annandale, Douglas and Idalia, and the Upper Ross suburbs of Kirwan, Kelso and Rasmussen, often have the yard space for a detached secondary dwelling. Bushland Beach and the northern suburbs are newer, while Mount Louisa can be more sloping.

Do I need approval for a granny flat?

Every new granny flat in Queensland needs building approval, and some also need planning approval from council, depending on the planning scheme and any overlays. See our QLD granny flat approvals guide.

Can I rent out a granny flat?

Yes. Since 26 September 2022, Queensland allows secondary dwellings to be rented to anyone. Extra fire and sound separation can apply when it's rented, so ask a certifier.
